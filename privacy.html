Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

The short version
  • Virtual try-on runs on your device. Those photos and camera frames never leave your phone or computer.
  • If you choose the AI color analysis, one resized photo is sent to our server and on to Anthropic's Claude API for that analysis only. Haeon doesn't save it.
  • Your saved shades, shopping list and settings stay in your own browser.
  • No accounts, no ads, no selling of personal information.

Who we are

Haeon is a K-beauty discovery website run by an independent developer in Toronto, Canada. We serve visitors in Canada and the United States.

Virtual try-on and live camera

When you upload a photo or turn on the camera in Try On or Compare, everything happens in your browser. Face-landmark detection (Google MediaPipe) and the makeup rendering run on your device. These images are not uploaded, stored or seen by us. The camera only turns on when you tap to start it.

AI personal color analysis (optional)

  • This feature only runs when you choose a photo and start the analysis. You can skip it and pick your season yourself.
  • Your browser first resizes the photo to 1024 pixels and saves it as a new JPEG, which removes location and camera information.
  • Our server function passes the photo to Anthropic's Claude API and returns a short result: season, lighting check and a few color notes. Our server doesn't write the photo to disk, a database or our logs.
  • Anthropic processes the photo to produce the result. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data isn't used to train its models, and inputs and outputs are deleted within 30 days unless a longer period is needed to enforce its usage policy or required by law. See Anthropic's retention page.
  • The analysis looks only at coloring (skin, eye and hair tones). Haeon doesn't identify people, doesn't create or keep facial recognition templates, and doesn't judge appearance or age.

What stays in your browser

Haeon uses your browser's local storage to remember your season, saved shades and looks, shopping list, skin-routine answers and shopping country. This data stays on your device; we can't see it. Clear it any time by clearing site data for Haeon in your browser settings.

Analytics and cookies

Haeon doesn't currently use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If we add privacy-friendly analytics to learn which features are used, we'll update this page first and describe what's collected.

Affiliate links

Some store links may be affiliate links. When you follow one, the retailer may use cookies to credit Haeon for a purchase. See our affiliate disclosure.

Children

Haeon isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't keep accounts or your photos, we generally hold no personal information we can link to you. Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to ask what we hold, correct it or delete it. Contact us and we'll respond.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the date at the top.
