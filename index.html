Your photo privacy details
  • Your photo is resized in your browser, which also removes its location and camera data.
  • It is sent over an encrypted connection to Gyeol's server, which passes it straight to our AI provider, Anthropic, for this one analysis. Gyeol's server doesn't save or log your photo.
  • Anthropic doesn't use it to train AI models and deletes it within 30 days, unless its usage policy or the law requires keeping it longer.
  • Your result (your season) is saved only in this browser, so your looks stay matched to you.
