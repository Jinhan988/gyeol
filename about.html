About Dalbom

Last updated November 13, 2026

Dalbom (달봄) joins two Korean words: dal, the moon, and bom, spring. We picked it for the soft, flattering light of a spring night, the kind of light that shows your true colors kindly. Dalbom is an independent K-beauty guide for shoppers in Canada and the United States.

What Dalbom does

  • Discover: five categories (Makeup, Skincare, Anti-Aging, Cleansing, Sun & Special Care), search and filters by brand, ingredient and skin concern.
  • Understand: key ingredients in plain English, how to use each product, when to use it and what to watch out for.
  • Experience: try real lip, blush and eye shades on your own face, with an optional AI personal color analysis you can always correct.
  • Shop: links to stores that sell the product in your country.

How we choose and describe products

  • Directions, key ingredients and cautions come from the brand's page or a major retailer's product page. Each product page links its source and the date we checked it.
  • Where we only have general guidance for a product type, we say so.
  • Products are never ranked by commission. Whether a store pays Dalbom has no effect on what we recommend or the order we show it.
  • Virtual try-on is a visual preview. Real color varies with lighting, camera and your natural coloring.
  • Dalbom isn't medical advice. For skin conditions, see a dermatologist.

Who runs Dalbom

Dalbom is built and run by an independent developer in Toronto, Canada. It isn't affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any brand or retailer shown on the site. Brand and product names belong to their owners.

How Dalbom is funded

Dalbom is free to use. It may earn a commission when you buy through some store links, at no extra cost to you. See our affiliate disclosure.
